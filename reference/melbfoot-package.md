# melbfoot: Explore Hourly Pedestrian Counts in Melbourne

Provides hourly pedestrian counts from the City of Melbourne Pedestrian
Counting System, summarised by sensor, year, day type and hour of day.
Includes helper functions to build typical-day profiles and to measure
how foot traffic changed relative to a baseline year, and a 'shiny' app
that helps business owners and event planners compare locations and
years.
